How to Use

  1. Read the runes. In the MUD, look at runes on oculus to see the seven runes arranged around the disc. Your goal is to get the colors in rainbow order — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet (roygbiv) — with every rune facing the same way. The oculus wraps around, so where the sequence starts doesn't matter.
  2. Enter the arrangement. Type the runes as one 7-letter string using the first letter of each color (R O Y G B I V), in the order they appear around the disc. Use uppercase when a rune points up, lowercase when it points down — e.g. rOygBiV.
  3. Solve. Click Solve (or press Enter). The tool searches up to 14 flips for a sequence that reaches the target — comfortably inside the game's 15-move limit before the oculus resets.
  4. Flip the runes. The result lists the runes to flip, in order. Flip each one in the MUD (e.g. flip red rune on oculus). Each flip reorients that rune and the runes reposition themselves.
  5. Orient the oculus. If the solution ends with the runes all pointing down (lowercase), flip oculus to turn the whole disc over — then enter the portal to complete the quest.

Reading the oculus can be confusing: you only see three runes at a time and rotating shifts the view by two. If the solver can't find a solution, double-check the order and orientation you entered.
